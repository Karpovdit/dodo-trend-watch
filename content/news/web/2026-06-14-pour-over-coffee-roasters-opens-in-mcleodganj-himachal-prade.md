After establishing itself across three locations in Delhi NCR, Pour Over Coffee Roasters (POCR) is embarking on a new journey, one that takes the brand from the energy of the city to the quiet intention of the mountains. The specialty coffee roaster has announced the opening of its newest outpost in McLeodganj, Himachal Pradesh, marking what founders Ritika Sharma and Aditya Sharma describe as a defining new chapter in the evolution of the brand, said a release.
