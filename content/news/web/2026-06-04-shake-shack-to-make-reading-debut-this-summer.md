Shake Shack is set to open its first Reading Shack this July, marking its 20th location in the UK. The New York-born burger chain will launch on Broad Street, giving customers in Reading their first opportunity to visit a physical Shake Shack restaurant after the brand was available via delivery in the town briefly in 2020.
