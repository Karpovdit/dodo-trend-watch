Restaurant Örme is set to relaunch with a new dining format next month, moving away from its previous tasting menu offering. The restaurant will introduce a social dining concept focused on sharing plates. The updated approach will centre on a relaxed menu structure featuring snacks, small plates and desserts designed for sharing, with regularly changing dishes inspired by modern British cooking. Restaurant Örme is located in Urmston, Manchester, and was founded in 2023 by Jack Fields, Tom Wilson and Rachel Roberts, with Fields and Wilson as directors and co-owners and Roberts as co-founder.
