Goa will renew existing beach shack licences for a fourth consecutive year, tourism minister Rohan Khaunte said, with renewals completed by 15 August on current terms. Stakeholder talks outlined tighter regulation, waste control, drone monitoring, Tourist Helpline 1364 displays and Wi‑Fi improvements, aiming to protect coastal ecology while enhancing visitor safety and transparency.
