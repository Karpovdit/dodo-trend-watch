The Tom Kerridge-backed #VATsTheProblem campaign has passed the 100,000-signature mark in just five days. At the time of writing, 111,881 people had signed the petition, which is calling for VAT on hospitality to be cut from 20% to 10%. Kerridge is asking business owners to encourage every member of their team to sign the petition, as well as their families and friends.
