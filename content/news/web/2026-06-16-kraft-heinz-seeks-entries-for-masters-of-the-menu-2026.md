Kraft Heinz is bringing back its nationwide cooking competition, Masters of the Menu, following its successful launch last year. This year’s edition features a larger prize pot, with the winner receiving £5,000 in cash, an expanded judging panel including 2025 competition winner Stefan Sewell, and a new partnership with London cookery school CORD by Le Cordon Bleu.
