As soccer fans gear up to watch the tournament over the next few weeks, all Jefferson’s and WingStand restaurants will celebrate the world’s favorite sport by offering 50% off boneless wings. (At WingStand, this includes their veggie wings as well.) The promotion will be available for the duration of the tournament, from June 11 – July 18.
