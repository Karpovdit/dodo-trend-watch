Spread across 1,680 square feet with seating for 69 guests, the Central 50 outlet reimagines the idea of a neighbourhood cafe. The space is brought to life through a soothing palette of terracotta and lime tones, layered with warm wooden finishes, brick-tiled elements, and rattan accents.
