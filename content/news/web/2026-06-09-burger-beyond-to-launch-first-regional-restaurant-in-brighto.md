Burger and steak restaurant Burger & Beyond is set to open in the former Patty & Bun site in Brighton for its first bricks-and-mortar restaurant outside of London. Set to open this summer, The cult burger brand will begin serving its award-winning patties from early July at its new site on Ship Street, a stone’s throw from the city’s seafront. The opening marks a return to the area for founders Tom Stock and Craig Povoas, who have strong family ties to Brighton and Sussex.
