At the centre of the concept is Akina​ - a well-travelled muse whose experiences across kitchens, markets, bars and homes have shaped the soul of the space. Rooted in Japanese culture yet deeply influenced by the world around her, Akina’s story unfolds through food, cocktails, design and the quiet intimacy of hosting, Built around the idea that “Stories deepen, when shared,” House of Akina moves away from cuisine labels and instead celebrates the emotional and sensory connections that food and hospitality create across cultures, said a release.
