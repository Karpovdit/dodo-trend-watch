Birmingham-based chef and restaurateur Andreas Antona has been appointed a Member of the Order of the British Empire (MBE) for services to the hospitality sector and charity. In an Instagram post, he said the honour was a ‘tremendous surprise’ and added that it recognised a career spent in an industry he has loved since childhood. Antona added: “British hospitality is going through very challenging times, but it is still the strong, powerful and diverse industry that I’ve always felt so strongly connected to.”
