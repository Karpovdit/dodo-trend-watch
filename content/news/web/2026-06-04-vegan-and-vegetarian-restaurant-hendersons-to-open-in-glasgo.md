Edinburgh vegan and vegetarian restaurant Hendersons will open a sister site in Glasgow this summer. Announced via Instagram, Hendersons Glasgow will be located at 721 Pollokshaws Road, near to Queens Park. The post revealed that Glasgow’s Southside area - which is a hotspot for independent restaurants - had been in the company’s sights for many years.
