The total operational cost for pubs and bars has risen by 48% between 2015 and 2025, with technology and software costs within the sector rising by 167%. This reflects a wider shift towards digital systems, including integrated POS platforms, AI-powered tools, and digital card machines, ‘accelerated by post-COVID digitisation’.
