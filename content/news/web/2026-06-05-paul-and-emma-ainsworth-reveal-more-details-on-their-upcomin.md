Paul and Emma Ainsworth have revealed more details about their relaunch of the St Enodoc hotel in Rock and set a 27 July opening date. Following their purchase of the property early last year, the pair - who run a string of restaurants in nearby Padstow including the Michelin-starred Paul Ainsworth at No 6 - will introduce two new restaurant concepts. The Estuary Grill will focus on Cornish produce from land and sea. The menu will include dry-aged meat from Philip Warren Butchers and locally caught fish and seafood sourced from Flying Fish.
