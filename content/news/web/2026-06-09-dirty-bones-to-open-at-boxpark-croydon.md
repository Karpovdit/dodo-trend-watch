The New-York-inspired restaurant brand Dirty Bones has announced it will open at Boxpark Croydon this June, as it celebrates its 15th anniversary year. According to the brand the opening marks the first of several developments planned for the business year. The new site is set to feature the chains signature menu including loaded fries, corn ribs and chicken wings, alongside chicken over rice and chicken over caesar dishes - inspired by New York bodegas - the Chopped Cheese Hero and freshly cooked rotisserie chicken.
