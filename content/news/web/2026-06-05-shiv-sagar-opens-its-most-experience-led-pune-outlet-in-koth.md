Located in one of Pune’s most culturally vibrant neighbourhoods, the Kothrud outlet has been designed as a homage to the city’s evolving food culture. Spread across a spacious 10,000 square feet bungalow with a seating capacity of 250 guests, the outlet spans three floors and combines earthy contemporary interiors, immersive dining experiences, and thoughtfully designed spaces for families, gatherings, and celebrations, said a release.
