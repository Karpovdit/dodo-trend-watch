Independent hospitality and event operator Bustler is set to open a new food hall in Derby. Bustler’s new St James Yard development is set to be made up of around 20 shipping containers arranged across two levels. The site will house chef-led food concepts, restaurants, bars, independent retail and service-led businesses, alongside indoor and outdoor social spaces.
