Bombae Apartment finds its space in Andheri’s fast pace, offering a place where one can slow down the moment they walk in. Inspired by modern apartments, the interiors are organized into zones like a living room, dining area, kitchen-style service counter and lounge spaces resembling the comfort of bedrooms. It’s home-inspired, making you feel you’ve arrived back in Bombay’s warm, earthy and mustard tones. The space respects the sensibilities of an old Bombay home, said a release.
