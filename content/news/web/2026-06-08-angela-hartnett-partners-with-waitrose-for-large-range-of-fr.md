Angela Hartnett MBE has partnered with Waitrose to launch a range of chilled Italian-inspired products. The project follows the success of the Murano chef’s Christmas collection last year, which was described as her first foray into supermarket products, branded as “Angela Hartnett with Waitrose”. Announcing the fresh collaboration on Instagram, the supermarket described the collection as a celebration of Italian cuisine, centred on ‘freshness, flavour and authenticity’.
