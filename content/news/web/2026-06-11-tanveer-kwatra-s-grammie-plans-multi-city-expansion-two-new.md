The first of the new brands is Double Crunch by GRAMMIE, a delivery only pizza and pasta brand that will start in South Delhi before extending across the wider NCR. The second is an in-house bakery and patisserie brand, positioned in the Italian rather than French tradition. Neighbors, the cafe and bar at Assagao in North Goa that Kwatra co-founded with his wife Ginny Kohli, is also looking to expand into Gurugram, said a release.
