Launching three cafes simultaneously marks one of the brand's largest city-entry milestones and signals its long-term commitment to the Kolkata market. With 220+ cafes across the country, Third Wave Coffee’s entry into Kolkata is a reflection of the growing demand for specialty coffee and premium café experiences across East India. As one of the region’s largest metropolitan centres and a city known for its rich heritage and vibrant cafe culture, Kolkata serves as a strategic gateway for the brand’s broader expansion plans in the region, said a release.
