Founded by hospitality entrepreneur Ayush Sahani, whose experience spans ventures such as Saltt at Oleander Farms, Karjat and 23 Bolo, Ministry of Naan is built around a simple yet often overlooked idea of North Indian food prepared with patience, precision, and consistency. While compact in format, the Bandra outlet reflects a clear culinary point of view, bringing together quality ingredients, careful craftsmanship, and an approachable neighbourhood dining experience, said a release.
