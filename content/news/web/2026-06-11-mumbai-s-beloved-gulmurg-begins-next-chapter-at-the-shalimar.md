Now, following an eighteen-month restoration, Gulmurg enters its next chapter. The reopening marks not a reinvention of the restaurant, but a continuation of everything that has made it a fixture of South Mumbai dining for more than sixty years. The refreshed space, renewed dining experience, and carefully considered additions to the menu have all been approached with a simple philosophy:evolve thoughtfully, without losing what people have always loved, said a release.
