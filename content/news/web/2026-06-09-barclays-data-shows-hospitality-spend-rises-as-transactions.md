Eating and drinking out recorded growth in total spend year on year, despite a decline in the number of transactions, according to new Barclays figures. The research, carried out by Opinium Research on behalf of Barclays, revealed overall hospitality spending increased in value by 0.8% year on year, while transaction volumes fell by 4%, highlighting a divergence between how much consumers are spending and how often they are visiting venues.
