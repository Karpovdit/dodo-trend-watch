Anirudh Kheny, managing partner of Keen Mustard Ventures has launched Heyou in Bengaluru, replacing Daysie to align with the city’s evolving F&B landscape. Located on MG Road, the venue features distinct bar and dining spaces, a revamped menu led by Asian small plates, and a sharper identity aimed at younger consumers, while reflecting key operational learnings from the group’s other existing brands.
