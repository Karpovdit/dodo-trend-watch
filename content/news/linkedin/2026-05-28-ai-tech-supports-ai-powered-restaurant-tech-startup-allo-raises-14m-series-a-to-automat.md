🍽️ #AI-powered restaurant #tech startup AllO raises $14M Series A to automate hospitality operations 🚀

Munich-based allO has secured $14M in Series A funding led by Zigg Capital, with participation from LifeX Ventures, @Aperture , Wecken & Cie. - Care4 AG & Cie., 20VC, and Keen Venture Partners.

AllO develops a unified restaurant management platform that combines POS systems, reservations, self-service kiosks, and delivery workflows into one AI-powered operating system for hospitality businesses. 🤖🍴

Its autonomous voice and vision agents can manage routine phone orders, automate inventory updates, and reduce manual operational tasks for restaurant operators in real time.

The company plans to use the funding to expand product engineering, scale operations, and accelerate adoption of its automated hospitality tools across the market.

As restaurants increasingly adopt AI and automation to improve efficiency and customer experience, AllO is positioning itself at the forefront of hospitality technology innovation.
