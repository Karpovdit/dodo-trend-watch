Influencers - what you do (fill your face, do voiceovers, stand in front of establishments) can now be replicated by technology - no more views or dollars for you. Why not contribute to society instead? Flavors of America’s storytelling (interviews with owners, real stories as we once knew how to tell and watch) are good for our collective cognitive health and social discourse - and there is no way to replicate an actual owner (because each is unique).
If you are not part of our solution - you will continue in your complicity of this problem.
