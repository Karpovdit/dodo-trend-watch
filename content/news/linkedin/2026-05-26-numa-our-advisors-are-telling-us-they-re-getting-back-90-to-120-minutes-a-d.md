"Our advisors are telling us they're getting back 90 to 120 minutes a day."

Derek Simonds joined Brian Kramer on The Kramer Show. They discussed what AI in a service drive looks like when it's built around the advisor.

When AI is woven into a dealership's systems and conversation history, it learns how an advisor actually works and starts handling the predictable parts: 
-the status updates
-the follow-ups
-the UVI upsells that used to die in a queue

Advisors who use it spend more of their day with the customer in the lane.
