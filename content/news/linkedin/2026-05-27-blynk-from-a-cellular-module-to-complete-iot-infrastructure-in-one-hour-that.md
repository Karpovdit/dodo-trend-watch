From a cellular module to complete IoT infrastructure in one hour. That's what the nRF9151 from Nordic Semiconductor + Blynk looks like.

One chip. Application processor, LTE-M/NB-IoT modem, and GNSS. Flash a pre-built binary, provision credentials over UART, and you're publishing live data to Blynk.

What you get on top of the hardware:
▪ Secure cloud infrastructure
▪ No-code dashboards and native mobile apps
▪ Fleet management and multi-tenant architecture for devices at scale
▪ OTA firmware updates

You're not just connecting a device. You're getting the full application layer.
WiFi works when there's infrastructure. Bluetooth works when there's a phone nearby. The nRF9151 works everywhere else.
