🫆🚀 The Power of IoT Sensors Is Transforming the Digital World 🌐⚡

IoT sensors are not just small electronic components — they are the foundation of modern smart technology.

From smart homes to industrial automation, these devices collect real-time data, improve efficiency, reduce waste, and enable faster, smarter decisions across multiple industries.

🔍 Inside this visual guide:

📡 Ultrasonic Distance Sensor
 Used to measure distance, detect objects, and support automation systems.

🌡️ Temperature Sensor
 Monitors thermal changes in environments, machines, servers, and industrial systems.

💧 Humidity Sensor
 Helps control humidity in smart homes, agriculture, warehouses, and sensitive environments.

👁️ PIR Motion Sensor
 Detects presence and movement, widely used in security, automatic lighting, and home automation.

🎨 Color Sensor
 Identifies colors in industrial processes, robotics, object classification, and quality control.

📍 Proximity Sensor
 Detects nearby objects without physical contact, essential for automatic systems and smart devices.

🧲 Hall Sensor
 Used to detect magnetic fields, rotation, position, and movement.

⚖️ Pressure & Altitude Sensor
 Important for atmospheric measurement, navigation, drones, wearables, and industrial applications.

📶 IR Distance & Break Beam Sensors
 Used for obstacle detection, object counting, and passage monitoring systems.
