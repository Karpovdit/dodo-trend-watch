AI agents aren’t just chatting—they’re rewiring SAP from the inside. 🤖  

While you’re approving POs manually, your competitor’s AI just closed 3 books before lunch.  
Most teams still treat SAP like a 2000s ERP—when it’s becoming an intelligent nervous system.  

Here’s where AI agents will hit hardest (and fastest):  
▶️ **Invoice Chaos → Auto-Resolution**: Agents read PDFs, match POs/GRNs, flag discrepancies *and* suggest fixes—no more 3-week AP backlogs.  
▶️ **Predictive Maintenance (SAP PM)**: Sensor data + SAP IoT → Agents predict failures *before* they halt production, auto-generating work orders in PM.  
▶️ **Talent Matching (SAP SuccessFactors)**: Agents scan internal skills, project needs, and learning paths to suggest *actual* internal gigs—not just job posts.  

This isn’t “AI for AI’s sake.” It’s about freeing your SAP superusers from spreadsheet hell to focus on strategy.
