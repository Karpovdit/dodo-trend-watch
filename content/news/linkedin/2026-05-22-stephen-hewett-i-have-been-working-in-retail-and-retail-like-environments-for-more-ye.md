I have been working in retail and retail like environments, for more years now than I care to count, but I can honestly say I never saw a technology that I thought could truly transform ways of working / create value in the wonderfully chaotic environments that are stores, restaurants and other travel or hospitality spaces.

The last 5 years building Ai native hybrid voice and app tools has totally changed my world view - you can now create fast, smart and intuitive ways for every frontline worker to gain access to the kind of knowledge and expertise that has historically been locked in the minds of your very best workers, access real time insight that have been locked behind systems that are nowhere near the shop floor, and offload mechanical data input/output tasks, liberating hard working front line workers from process and clunky tech, letting them focus on what they do best - meeting customers where they are, delivering brilliant experience, running awesome operations.
