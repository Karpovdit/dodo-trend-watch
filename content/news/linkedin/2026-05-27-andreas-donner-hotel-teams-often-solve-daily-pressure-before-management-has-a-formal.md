Hotel teams often solve daily pressure before management has a formal AI strategy.

A reservation employee drafts guest replies with AI.
A sales manager uses AI to prepare offer text.
Front office teams test translation tools.
Guest inquiries are summarised, copied, rephrased or pre-qualified with tools nobody has officially approved.

This is where Shadow AI starts.

The problem is not that hotel teams use AI.

The problem is that nobody may know:
• which guest data is being entered
• which tools are being used
• which workflows are affected
• who is responsible
• where operational risk begins

For hotels, AI readiness should not start with buying another tool.

It should start with a simple operational question:

Where is AI already touching reservations, guest communication, offers, calls and intake workflows — and how do we bring it under control?

Peak Care AI helps hotels move from uncontrolled AI experiments to practical, structured and useful AI workflows.
