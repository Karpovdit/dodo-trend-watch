Restaurant friends... want the fastest, cheapest way to bring in customers? Fix your online menu. Here's my 10-min audit 👀

1. Google Biz Profile:  The definitive digital front door to your restaurant ...91% of guests look up a menu online before visiting. 

2. Yelp: like it or not, mis-matched menus (incldg old pricing and photos) trigger negative reviews. *Fix the menu and respond to every review.

3. Delivery apps: (DoorDash/ Uber Eats) these are the new search engines. 55% of 1st time orders come from casual browsers. New big stat.

AI is officially in the mix. 22% of diners now use it to choose a restaurant... ☝ and this is the list that the AI engines are using to pull in their data.

Menu management IS marketing. Don't skip it.

Every Circle 4 Marketing, LLC engagement starts with a foundational diagnostic for a reason. Fix the (digital) front door before you drive traffic to it.
