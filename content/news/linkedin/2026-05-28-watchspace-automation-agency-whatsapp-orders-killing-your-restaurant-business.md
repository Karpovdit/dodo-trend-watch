WHATSAPP orders killing your restaurant business?

David runs Mama's Kitchen in Wandegeya. Every evening, his phone buzzes non-stop with WhatsApp orders. Customers want rice and beans, others want chicken stew, some ask for delivery to Makerere. By 9 PM, David is exhausted just from typing responses.

Last month, David's restaurant was losing 15 orders daily because he couldn't respond fast enough. Customers were going to competitors who replied instantly.

Now? David uses automated WhatsApp ordering. When customers message "menu", they instantly get the full menu with prices. When they type "order rice and chicken", the system captures their details, confirms the order, and sends cooking time. David's team gets organized order slips automatically.

Results after 3 weeks: 47 more orders daily, zero missed messages, and David actually enjoys evenings again. His revenue jumped from 800,000 to 1.2 million shillings monthly.

The system works while David focuses on cooking quality food instead of typing the same responses 200 times daily.
