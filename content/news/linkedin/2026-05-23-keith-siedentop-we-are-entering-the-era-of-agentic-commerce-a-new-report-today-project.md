We are entering the era of "Agentic Commerce." A new report today projects that autonomous AI agents will drive over $1 trillion in U.S. commerce by 2030. This shift moves us beyond simple e-commerce toward a world where AI agents proactively manage replenishment, negotiate prices, and orchestrate complex purchasing decisions on behalf of consumers and businesses alike.
