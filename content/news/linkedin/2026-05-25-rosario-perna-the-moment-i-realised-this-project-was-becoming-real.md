The moment I realised this project was becoming “real”…

People no longer just sit down for dinner.
They interact with the space.
They film it.
They become part of it.

And that’s probably the most exciting part.

Still far from the final vision, but seeing guests naturally react to the immersive elements while simply enjoying a steak dinner…
that’s when the experience stops being “just projection” and starts becoming part of the dinner itself.

Even Kubo, originally just a visual effect, is starting to change the energy of the table.
Phones come out.
People lean in.
Everyone shares the same moment together.

Work in progress.
