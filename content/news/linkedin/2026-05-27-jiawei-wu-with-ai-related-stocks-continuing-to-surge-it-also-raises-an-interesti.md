With AI related stocks continuing to surge, it also raises an interesting question:

How will AI continue to transform retail and restaurant operations?

A few areas immediately come to mind:

1）AI can potentially analyze weather, holidays, local events, and transaction patterns to help merchants better prepare staffing schedules and inventory ahead of busy periods.

2）Self-order kiosks are already common today, but most systems still lack personalized recommendations that human servers naturally provide. Future AI ordering systems may eventually suggest meals, combos, and promotions based on customer preferences and habits.

3）Payments are already becoming faster through tap technology, but biometric payments such as facial recognition or fingerprint authentication may push checkout experiences even further.

The future of retail may become a combination of AI, personalization, operational intelligence, and invisible payments.
