The shift in today’s workforce is not always about layoffs. It is about evolution. 🧠

As #ITSMFMember Alicia Lyttle shares, many companies are not eliminating roles. They are redefining them. Entry level positions across customer service, administrative support, marketing, and analysis are being reshaped as AI becomes more integrated into everyday work.

The real difference is in how people respond.

Those who understand how to leverage AI tools are accelerating their growth and positioning themselves for what’s next. Building these skills is no longer optional. It is a clear advantage in a changing landscape.

The question is how you are preparing to stay ahead.
