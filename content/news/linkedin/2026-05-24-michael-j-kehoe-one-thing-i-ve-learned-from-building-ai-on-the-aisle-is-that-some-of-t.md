One thing I’ve learned from building AI On The Aisle is that some of the most important conversations in AI aren’t necessarily about the newest tools — they’re about separating meaningful innovation from noise.

The recent article I published on “AI washing” became the highest-read article on the newsletter so far, generated new followers, and sparked some great conversations around what real AI transformation actually looks like in retail operations.

I appreciate everyone who has been following along on this journey.

What continues to stand out to me is that professionals across retail, operations, supply chain, and technology are looking for grounded conversations about AI — not just headlines, but practical discussions around execution, visibility, labor, inventory, and operational intelligence.

That’s exactly the direction I want AI On The Aisle to continue moving toward.

Thank you again to everyone reading, engaging, and contributing to the discussion. More to come.
