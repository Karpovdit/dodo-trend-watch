Most B2B companies think AI is just a chatbot for writing emails. Elite revenue teams know better. They are using agentic AI to completely transform their Go To Market operations.



If you are a VP of Sales or a RevOps leader, here is an elite look at what tools like Claude Code can actually do for your organisation right now.

a. Produce comprehensive competitor intelligence. Claude Code can pull data from multiple sources to generate 60-page reports covering digital presence, paid media, and strategic positioning.

b. Create Customer Avatar Councils. Imagine a virtual panel of expert personas representing your Ideal Customer Profile. You can use this AI panel to evaluate your messaging and website effectiveness before you launch a campaign.

c. Automate your RevOps data. You can drop a CSV into the system to get instant statistics and correlations. It can even connect directly to your data sources to build daily automated performance dashboards.
Generate client-ready proposals. The AI can instantly build branded proposals, complete with customised pricing and timelines.

The goal of agentic AI is never to replace your sales team. The goal is to eliminate the hours they spend on preparation and manual research. When you remove that burden, your reps can focus entirely on building relationships and driving revenue.
