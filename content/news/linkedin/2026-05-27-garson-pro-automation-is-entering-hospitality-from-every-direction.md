Automation is entering hospitality from every direction:

QR menus, self-ordering, AI chatbots, service robots, smart kitchens, automated check-in, and more.

But hospitality is not only a transaction.
A restaurant visit is not just about receiving food.
A hotel stay is not just about receiving a room key.

Guests also expect attention, confidence, help, atmosphere, trust, and sometimes simple human contact.

That is why the best model is not “human vs automation.”

It is:
Automation for repetitive tasks.
Humans for care, judgment, trust, and emotion.

In our new Garson.Pro story, we explore where automation helps hotels and restaurants — and where it can go too far.
