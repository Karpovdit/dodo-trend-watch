🤖 AI isn't replacing recruiters—it's transforming what recruiting can be.

The latest shift in talent acquisition is moving from GenAI (tools you prompt) to Applied AI (agents that work autonomously). Imagine this:

✓ A Sourcing Agent continuously identifies candidates 24/7
✓ A Screening Agent conducts fair assessments beyond business hours  
✓ A Scheduling Agent orchestrates complex interviews across timezones
✓ An Interview Agent provides live guidance for consistency

Recruiters still lead. But instead of spending 30 hours/week on sourcing, they now focus on what matters: building relationships with top talent, making strategic hiring decisions, and serving as true talent partners to their organizations.

According to Phenom's 2026 AI Recruiting Guide, 35-45% of companies have already adopted AI in hiring, and the sector is projected to expand at 6.17% annually.

The competitive advantage isn't choosing between human or AI—it's knowing how to combine both strategically.

How are you leveraging AI to enhance (not replace) your recruiting strategy?
