🚀 Startup Spotlight 🚀
allO - AI-native all-in-one operating system for restaurants, unifying POS, payments, reservations, delivery, and back-office with AI-powered digital employees.

🔍 Company Overview
- CEO: Cancan Liu
- Year Founded: 2020
- Location: Munich, Germany
- Team Size: 51-200
- Webpage: https://allo.restaurant

🎯 What They Do
- One-line Description: AI-native all-in-one operating system for restaurants, unifying POS, payments, reservations, delivery, and back-office with AI-powered digital employees.
- Target Customers: Independent and small-to-medium restaurant owners in Europe, particularly ethnic cuisine restaurants (70% of Germany's restaurants) that have been underserved by legacy POS systems.
- Industry Verticals: AI in Retail / E-commerce

🚀 Achievements & Status
- Current Stage/Status: Series A
- Major Milestones: Series A
- Key Investors: 2020: Company founded; 2022: €1.4M Pre-Seed from Keen Venture Partners; 2023: Red Dot Design Award; 2024: $5M Seed led by 20VC, 200+ locations; 2025: Expanded to 1,000+ locations; 2026: €12M ($14M) Series A led by Zigg Capital, launched AI Reservation & Ordering Voice Agent
- Basic Growth Metrics: 1,000+ active restaurant locations (6x growth since Seed round); Revenue 3.5x YoY; 30% of new customers from referrals; 10x ARR growth in 2023-2024; 4x ARPU growth in 2023-2024
