Spotlight: The use of AI in food NPD is changing

Seeking an edge, food manufacturers are looking at different ways of using AI in their NPD processes.

AI is helping to shake up the development of new food products, reducing the number of lines that fail commercially amid fierce competition to tap into fast-evolving consumer trends.

And agentic AI is coming to the fore.

This feature includes insight from:

Simon Hayward, VP international sales at US-based software services company Freshworks

Patrick Young at PRS IN VIVO
