Running a busy restaurant means managing chaos in real-time. When tickets are flying, missing a single side of fries or an extra pickle can disrupt the guest experience.
I needed a solution that fit our exact workflow at BK★JANI, so I built it.
We designed and deployed a fully customizable Kitchen Display System (KDS) built on top of our Square POS. By tailoring the tech to the physical line, we've minimized human error, optimized ticket times, and given the kitchen total clarity.
Built by operators, for operators.
