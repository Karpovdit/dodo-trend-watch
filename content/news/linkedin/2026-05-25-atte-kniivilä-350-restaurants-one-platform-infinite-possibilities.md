🥪 350+ restaurants. One platform. Infinite possibilities.

We are proudly announcing our partnership with Nordic Bites Group – Yonoton becomes the exclusive digital partner for 350+ Subway restaurants across Finland, Sweden, Norway, and Denmark.
This isn't just another integration deal. This is omnichannel at scale.
What happens when you connect mobile ordering, self-service kiosks, POS systems, delivery partners and kitchen management through a single cloud platform? You stop thinking in silos. You start thinking in systems.
Menu changes cascade instantly across all touchpoints. Customer data flows where it's needed. Campaigns hit the right people at the right time. Operations become predictable.
Nordic Bites Group saw what we build and trusted us with 350 restaurants. That's not a small bet.
The rollout already started in Denmark. Sweden is ongoing, Norway, and Finland will follow. By the end of this year, thousands of customers will experience what true omnichannel actually feels like.
This is what we do at Yonoton: we make complexity disappear so restaurants can focus on what matters – delighting customers and growing revenue.
Grateful to Petteri Lehtimäki and the team at Nordic Bites for the partnership. Can't wait to show what's possible.
