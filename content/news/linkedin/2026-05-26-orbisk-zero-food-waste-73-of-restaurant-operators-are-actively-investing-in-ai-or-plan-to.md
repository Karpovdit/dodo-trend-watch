73% of restaurant operators are actively investing in AI or plan to start in 2026.
Only 5% report measurable operational impact so far.

That gap isn't a technology problem. It's a deployment problem.
Most AI in hospitality right now is chasing the wrong outcomes, chatbots, reservation tools, marketing automation. Useful, maybe. But the kitchen, where 30–40% of a site's cost lives, is still largely running on gut feel and end-of-shift guesswork.

The operators who will close that gap aren't the ones with the most tools. They're the ones who've pointed AI at the right problem: what's actually being wasted, in real time, before the P&L takes the hit.

The data on this is clear. The execution isn't.

📎 Source: BusinessWire industry survey, 2026
