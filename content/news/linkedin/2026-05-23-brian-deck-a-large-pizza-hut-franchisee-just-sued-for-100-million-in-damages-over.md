A large Pizza Hut franchisee just sued for $100 million in damages over an AI rollout. The AI didn't malfunction. It worked exactly as designed. That's what makes this story worth understanding.

In September 2021, Yum! Brands Brands acquired Dragontail Systems for $72 million USD. The promise: a "super manager." An algorithm that would optimize kitchen flow, time every order, and ensure a pizza was never waiting on the rack for a driver to show up. Fast forward to 2024 and Yum Brands mandated a full system rollout across its franchise network. After roughly one year, a major 111-location franchisee operator alleged the AI implementation destroyed over $100 million in enterprise value through collapsed delivery performance and cratered sales. On May 6, 2026, Chaac Pizza Northeast filed the lawsuit in the Texas Business Court.

This isn't an AI failure story. It's an implementation failure story. And the pattern is showing up far beyond restaurants.
