In March 2026, this started as a simple restaurant ordering app built with Flutter.

A few months later, it evolved into a complete restaurant-tech ecosystem.

Instead of stopping at a customer-facing application, I wanted to explore and build the entire operational workflow behind modern restaurant systems — from ordering and kitchen management to rider coordination and admin operations.

Currently building under Orbit Innovations :

• Customer Ordering App
• POS System
• Kitchen Display System (KDS)
• Riders App
• Admin Dashboard

All applications are connected through a centralized Supabase architecture with realtime synchronization and optimized backend performance across the ecosystem.

Key areas of focus throughout development:
• scalable system architecture
• realtime operational syncing
• intuitive UI/UX design
• seamless workflow integration
• performance-focused infrastructure
• modern restaurant operations

The project is currently in the development and testing phase, with continuous improvements being made across user experience, analytics, synchronization, and operational reliability.

What started as a UI experiment gradually became one of the most valuable learning experiences for me in:
• Flutter development
• product design
• system thinking
• full-stack architecture
• realtime systems
• operational ecosystem building

Huge thanks to my co-founders, Sayan Sarkar  and SAKSHI SAHA , for their constant support throughout this journey.
