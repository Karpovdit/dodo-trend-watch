The founding insight at allO is worth saying plainly: restaurant owners want the work done. They do not want to learn another piece of software.

That single observation shapes everything. POS, payments, reservations, delivery, inventory, menu updates all handled by AI agents that act without requiring the owner to manage them. The system learns the restaurant. The restaurant runs.

$14M Series A led by Zigg Capital, with LifeX Ventures, Aperture, Wecken & Cie, 20VC, and Keen Venture Partners. Elizabeth Chrystal, former CFO of Momofuku, joins the board. Matt Baumgartner , ex-product director at Toast, joins the advisory board.

1,000-plus active restaurant locations in Germany. 6x location growth since seed. 3x revenue growth. European expansion next.

Founded 2020 in Munich. Pandemic-era scan-to-order product that grew into a full operating system.
