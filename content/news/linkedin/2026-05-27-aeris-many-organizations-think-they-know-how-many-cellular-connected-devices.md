Many organizations think they know how many cellular-connected devices they have in the field. Then they start digging - and discover the real number may be dramatically higher. But the bigger risk isn’t just more devices. It’s more devices communicating in ways no one is monitoring, managed across siloed teams, and sitting outside a unified security strategy. 

In this clip from Episode 13 of “IoT Real Talk,” Suraj Chandrasekaran of Palo Alto Networks explains why wireless IoT has become one of the most overlooked exposure points in enterprise security. Because when you don’t know what’s connected, you don’t know what’s exposed.
