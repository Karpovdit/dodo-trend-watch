AI shopping agents are transforming commerce, shifting consumer behavior from "click-to-buy" to "delegate-to-buy."

By 2030, agentic payments could drive hundreds of billions in online sales. But delegating purchase power requires a massive leap in confidence. The payment moment is the ultimate trust chokepoint. Without a modernized infrastructure to verify agent identity and enforce spending mandates, payment providers risk increased fraud and loss of market share.

For institutions looking to lead in the coming era of agentic commerce, one thing is clear: You must build the right trust layer to support AI-driven transactions securely.
