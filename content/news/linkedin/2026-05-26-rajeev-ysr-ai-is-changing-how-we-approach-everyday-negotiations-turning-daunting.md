AI is changing how we approach everyday negotiations, turning daunting tasks into manageable data points. A recent example showcases a driver who faced a massive 6-lakh rupee car service estimate and used AI to audit the bill by differentiating between essential repairs and unnecessary dealer upsells.

Here is how strategic prompting helped:
*   Clarified specific car issues versus general suggestions.
*   Identified duplicate billing and inflated labor costs.
*   Compared parts pricing against current market rates.

By simply uploading the estimate and asking for a detailed breakdown, the total bill dropped to 2.5 lakh rupees. It turns out that a little bit of technical savvy can save you a fortune in unexpected places.
