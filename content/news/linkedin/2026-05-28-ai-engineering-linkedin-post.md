iFixAi is an open-source diagnostic that runs up to 32 inspections against any AI agent to test for operational AI misalignment, covering fabrication, manipulation, deception, unpredictability, and opacity. The Pizza Hut Dragontail lawsuit is a real example of what happens when that alignment layer breaks down.

Chaac Pizza Northeast is suing Pizza Hut over Dragontail, its AI delivery dispatcher. Drivers got visibility into kitchen queues and tip amounts, cherry-picked high-tip orders, and NYC sales swung from +10% to nearly -10% because the governance layer around the model had no controls, no overrides, and no escalation path.

Using only the public lawsuit data, the team built a fixture and ran 6 of the 32 inspections against it.
