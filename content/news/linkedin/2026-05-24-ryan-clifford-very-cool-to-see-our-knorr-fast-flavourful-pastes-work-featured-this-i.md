Very cool to see our Knorr Fast & Flavourful Pastes work featured! This is just the beginning of how AI is reshaping food innovation.

What I love about this example is the symmetry - we set out to help people unlock fast, flavourful cooking at home, and AI helped us do exactly that behind the scenes: move faster and create even better tasting products.

For me, the real value is the time AI unlocks for other work - more time to focus on creativity, and to truly understand the real cooking tensions we’re solving for. Without these tools, we’d likely still be in the lab…
