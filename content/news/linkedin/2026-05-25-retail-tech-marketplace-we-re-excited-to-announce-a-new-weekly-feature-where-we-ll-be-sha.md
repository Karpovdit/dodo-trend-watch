💻 WEEKLY RETAIL TECH DIGEST - 19th May
 
 🤖 AI & AUTOMATION
 • Alibaba Group - Focuses on AI growth despite a decline in income, indicating a strategic pivot towards technology investment.
 • Amazon - Discontinues the Rufus chatbot in favor of a new AI assistant, Alexa for Shopping, emphasizing integrated AI solutions in ecommerce.
 • WHSmith - Launches a new AI-powered retail assistant designed to help travellers make quicker purchasing decisions in airport stores.
 • IKEA - Tests robotic boots called Moonwalkers that utilize AI technology to enhance fulfilment speed.
 • Tapestry - Patents new AI data analytics technology aimed at reducing the time required for data analysis.
 
 💳 PAYMENTS
 • Stripe - Partners with Google to enhance agentic checkout across major AI platforms, streamlining the checkout process for online retailers.
 • Lidl in Germany - Expands its self-scanning trial, 'Lidl & Go', to seven additional stores, allowing customers to monitor spending in real time.
