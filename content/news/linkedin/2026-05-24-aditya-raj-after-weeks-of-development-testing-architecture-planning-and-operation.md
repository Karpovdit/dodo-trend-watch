After weeks of development, testing, architecture planning, and operational design, I finally built DineFlow — a fully production-ready SaaS restaurant operating system designed for real-world restaurant environments.

DineFlow digitizes the complete dining workflow, from customer ordering to kitchen execution and waiter coordination.

The platform includes:

• QR-based table ordering system
• AI-powered VoiceOrder Genie for voice food ordering
• Live Kitchen Display System (KDS)
• Reception and waiter management terminals
• Real-time order tracking
• Multi-tenant SaaS architecture
• Restaurant analytics dashboard
• Subscription licensing system
• Live synchronization across all terminals
• Staff and table management system

One feature I particularly enjoyed building was the Progressive Web App (PWA) experience for customers.

The customer menu behaves almost like a native mobile application directly inside the browser. Guests can scan a QR code, open the menu instantly, add the app to their home screen, use branded splash screens, persistent sessions, optimized mobile navigation, and experience near-app-level responsiveness without downloading anything from an app store.

I also configured the complete PWA environment manually, including:
• Dynamic favicon systems
• App icons for different devices and resolutions
• Browser manifest configuration
• Splash screen assets
• Mobile install support
• Theme-color integration
• Cross-device compatibility optimization
