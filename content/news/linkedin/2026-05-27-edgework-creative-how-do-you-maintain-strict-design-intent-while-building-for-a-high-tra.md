How do you maintain strict design intent while building for a high-traffic hospitality space? 🥃☕
We recently wrapped up a stunning custom fabrication project for the new John G. Carlisle Cafe at the historic Buffalo Trace Distillery, and we’re breaking down the details on the blog.
Projects like this require an integrated approach. By handling the architectural millwork, custom furniture , retail fixtures and metal fabrication under one roof, we ensured flawless execution and material consistency across the entire space.
Project highlights:
Custom Tables + Banquettes: Slabs of character-rich live-edge oak and custom laminate diner style tabels paired with custom metal bases.
Integrated Retail Fixtures: Floor-to-ceiling brass and floating walnut  shelving with integrated lighting.
Architectural Elements: Fluted glass and brushed brass service partitions alongside walnut cabinetry.

A huge thank you to the design and construction teams for trusting our shop to bring this vision to life. Read the full blog post to see how we built these multi-material pieces for maximum commercial durability.
