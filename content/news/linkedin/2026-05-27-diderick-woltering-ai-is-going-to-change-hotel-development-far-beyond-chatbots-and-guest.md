AI is going to change hotel development far beyond chatbots and guest apps.

The real impact is happening behind the scenes in pre-opening, renovations, project coordination, and operations.

Imagine fewer delays, better visibility across budgets, suppliers, timelines, approvals, and installations.

AI won’t replace hospitality professionals,but it will help experienced teams work faster, smarter, and with less chaos.

I believe the future of hotel development will combine:

• human experience
• operational understanding
• smarter technology
• better data visibility

The companies that adapt early will have a major advantage — not just in efficiency, but in delivering better openings and smoother projects overall.
At the end of the day, hotels are still built by people.

AI just helps keep everyone on the same page… hopefully before the opening countdown reaches panic mode.

Curious how others in hospitality, construction, and development see AI shaping the future of projects.
