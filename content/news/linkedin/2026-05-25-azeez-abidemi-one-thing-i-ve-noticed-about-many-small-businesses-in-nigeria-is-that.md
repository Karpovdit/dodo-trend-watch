One thing I’ve noticed about many small businesses in Nigeria is that they don’t usually lose customers because their product is bad…

They lose customers because nobody responds fast enough. 📞

I recently worked on a project for a real restaurant in Abuja called Mama Tee’s Kitchen, and the problem was painfully simple:
During busy hours, staff couldn’t answer incoming calls. Customers trying to ask about the menu, delivery or reservations would get no response and move on.

So I built an AI-powered business concierge that answers every call 24/7 in Nigerian English like a real staff member. 🤖🍲

The system now:
• Takes food orders automatically
• Handles table reservations instantly
• Answers customer questions naturally
• Routes calls to the right department
• Logs orders, complaints and reservations into Airtable automatically
• Creates Google Calendar booking events
• Sends instant Telegram notifications to the Kitchen, Bookings and Customer Care teams
• Connects to a premium restaurant website with live AI voice + WhatsApp support

What excites me most is the real-life impact.

Mama Tee’s Kitchen no longer misses customer calls, staff no longer do repetitive manual logging, and the business now operates with the efficiency of a tech company while still serving authentic Nigerian home cooking.

And honestly, it goes beyond restaurants; this same system can help salons, clinics, hotels and small businesses across Africa

Thousands of businesses globally lose customers…
