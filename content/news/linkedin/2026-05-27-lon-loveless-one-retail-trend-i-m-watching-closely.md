One retail trend I’m watching closely:
Customers are increasingly arriving in stores already “AI informed.”
They’ve already:
━━ 🤖 Compared products with AI tools
━━ 📊 Read summaries and reviews
━━ 🔍 Asked AI assistants for recommendations
━━ 🛒 Narrowed their options before entering the store
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
At first glance, this sounds like a threat to in-store retail teams.
I actually think it changes the role — not eliminates it.
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
The opportunity now becomes:
━━ 🎯 Building trust
━━ 🏬 Demonstrating products live
━━ 🔄 Translating complexity into real-world use cases
━━ 🤝 Helping customers feel confident in the final decision
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
In high-consideration categories like consumer tech, the human interaction may actually become MORE important at the final decision point.
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
The retailers and partners that win won’t just deploy AI.
They’ll prepare field teams for AI-informed shoppers.
