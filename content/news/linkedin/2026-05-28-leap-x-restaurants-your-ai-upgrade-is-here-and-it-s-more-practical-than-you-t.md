🚨 Restaurants, Your AI Upgrade Is Here — and It's More Practical Than You Think.

Most restaurant operators hear "AI" and picture robots or sci-fi tech that's years away.

Crunchtime just proved that's wrong.

Their May 2026 suite update is live — and it's built around one thing: making AI work on the floor, right now, for real operators.

Here's what's turning heads:

🎙️ Voice-Based Inventory — Managers count stock 3-4x faster using AI-powered speech-to-text. No clipboards. No fat-finger errors. Just speed.

📊 Staffing Template Assistant — Cross-checks labor templates against actual revenue data to build demand-driven schedules. Less guesswork. Better margins.

🎥 Just-in-Time Training — Instructional videos embedded directly into operational forms. Employees get guidance exactly when and where they need it.

📈 Operations Scorecard — Store managers get instant visibility into how their location stacks up on inventory variance, labor, and net sales. Accountability made simple.

And this wasn't built in a boardroom vacuum.

Crunchtime spoke with 1,000+ operators at the National Restaurant Association Show before shipping these features.

That's the difference between software that looks good in a demo and software that actually gets used on a Tuesday night rush.

The restaurant industry has always been about speed, consistency, and people.

AI isn't replacing any of that — it's finally making all of it easier.
