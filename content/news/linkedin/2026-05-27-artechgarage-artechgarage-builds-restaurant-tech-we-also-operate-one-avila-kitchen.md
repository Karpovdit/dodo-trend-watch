ArTechGarage builds restaurant tech. We also operate one (Avila Kitchen, soft-launching in Miami). That gave us a clear view from the operator side of what actually moves the needle in a small restaurant — and what every vendor sells you that nobody actually uses.

Here's what genuinely pays off:

A clean website. Loads under 2 seconds, menu visible without clicks, phone tappable.

WhatsApp AI for orders and reservations. Deflects 50 to 70% of repetitive messages.

A menu the owner can update without calling a developer.

One round of real food photography. $300 to $600, one time, lasts years.

What to skip: self-order kiosks, AI demand prediction, loyalty apps with their own login, table-side tablets. None of those move the needle for a 40-seat restaurant.

Realistic total to go fully digital: $2,000 to $5,000 one-time. Not the $20K agencies quote — and we know because we ran our own build at that number.
