Maximize Your Revenue and Enhance Customer Experience with Self-Service Kiosks!

According to a recent study, 1 in 3 consumers prefer using a self-service kiosk over ordering at the counter even if the queue is the same. 💬

✅ Increase Revenue: Self-service kiosks allow you to serve more customers simultaneously, thus increasing your revenue. With integrated suggestion modules, you can also boost your customers' average spend. According to a study at McDonald's, customers spend an average of 30% more on a kiosk than at the counter.
✅ Improve Customer Experience: Less time spent waiting to order and pay means greater customer satisfaction. Kiosks offer an immersive experience with modern and intuitive interfaces, ensuring smooth navigation and secure payment.
✅ Reduce Stress and Error Risks: By streamlining the ordering process, self-service kiosks allow your team to work more calmly and limit errors during order taking.
✅ Increase Customer Loyalty: Integrate an online loyalty program to encourage customers to return. Personalized suggestions and rewards unlocked via kiosks strengthen customer loyalty.
✅ Enhance Your Customer Insight: Collect valuable data on your customers' consumption habits through interactions on the kiosks. Use this information to offer personalized suggestions and stimulate consumption.
✅ Attract New Customers: Self-service kiosks appeal to busy local customers, thus avoiding frustration associated with long queues.
