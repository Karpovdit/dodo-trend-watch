Self service kiosks are not just a big chain thing anymore.

For a lot of restaurants, they are a simple way to:
- keep the line moving with fewer hands
- reduce order mistakes
- raise average ticket with smart add ons

But here is the part most people miss.

If the POS is not set up right, kiosks can turn into a mess fast.

3 things we look for before you add kiosks:
1) Modifiers and combos are clean and easy to tap
2) Kitchen routing is dialed in so tickets land in the right place
3) Payments, tips, and receipts are simple for guests and staff
