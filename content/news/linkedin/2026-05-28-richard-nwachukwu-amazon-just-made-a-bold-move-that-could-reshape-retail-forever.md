🚀 Amazon just made a bold move that could reshape retail forever.

They're offering competitors access to "Alexa for Shopping"—an AI shopping assistant service built on AWS. Think about that for a second: Amazon is literally selling the tools that could compete with Amazon.

✓ Retailers can now deploy AI-powered shopping assistants on their own sites
✓ Amazon monetizes its AI infrastructure while expanding AWS enterprise reach
✓ The retail landscape shifts from "who has the best AI?" to "who has the best AI infrastructure?"

This is classic Amazon strategy: become the platform, not just the player. They're betting that controlling the infrastructure layer is more valuable than owning individual retail experiences.

The real question? Will retailers embrace this, or will they see it as giving Amazon a window into their operations?
