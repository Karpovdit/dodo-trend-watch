allO is betting that restaurants do not need another SaaS tab. They need an operator that can act across the whole daily stack.

The Munich company raised a EUR 12M Series A led by Zigg Capital, with participation from LifeX Ventures, Aperture® Venture Capital, Wecken & Cie. - Care4 AG, 20VC, and Keen Venture Partners.

The product already spans POS, integrated payments, reservations, kiosks, delivery, webshop, and back office. allO is now layering AI-powered digital employees on top, starting with a Reservation and Ordering Agent that answers calls and pushes reservations and takeaway orders directly into the system.

That breadth is the important part. Restaurants are not short on point tools. They are short on time, margin, and staff, while owners still stitch together ordering, delivery, menus, reconciliation, payments, reservations, and supplier workflows.

allO says it serves more than 1,000 active restaurant locations across Germany. Since its seed round, locations are up 6x, revenue is up 3.5x year-on-year, and 30% of new customers now come through referrals. The next planned agents target inventory and menu updates.
